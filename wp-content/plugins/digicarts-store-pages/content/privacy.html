This policy explains how DigiCarts handles personal information when you browse our digital-products store, place an order, create an account or contact us. For privacy questions, email support@digicarts.co.

1. Information we collect

  • Orders: name, email, phone number, products purchased, order status, amounts, coupon information and any optional address or order notes you supply.
  • Accounts: account identifiers, profile details and a securely hashed password. Guest checkout is available, so creating an account is optional.
  • Payments: payment method, transaction references and payment/refund status. When a payment gateway is used, it handles the payment credentials under its own terms. Do not send us card numbers, CVVs or UPI PINs.
  • Support: your name, email, selected topic, optional order reference and the message you submit.
  • Technical activity: information such as IP address, browser information, session identifiers, download-access records and server logs used to operate and protect the store.

2. Why we use it

We use relevant information to fulfil orders, provide eligible downloads, administer accounts, process payments or refunds, respond to enquiries, prevent misuse and maintain necessary business records. Your phone number is collected at checkout for order-related contact; providing it does not subscribe you to promotional messages.

We do not treat an order or support request as permission for unrelated marketing. If optional marketing is introduced, we will explain the choice and how to withdraw it.

3. Cookies and browser storage

The store uses essential cookies or similar storage for login, cart/session continuity, security and relevant interface preferences. These support actions such as keeping items in your cart or remembering an authenticated session. Browser settings can restrict or delete storage, but doing so may interrupt checkout or sign-in.

Optional analytics or advertising features, if introduced, will be described in an updated notice with any consent choices required by law.

4. Who may receive information

Access is limited to people and providers who need information for the relevant task, such as store administration, hosting, payment processing, email delivery, technical support and professional advice. The providers involved depend on the services enabled for your transaction. They may operate in India or other countries.

We may disclose information when legally required, to address fraud or security incidents, or to protect lawful rights. We do not sell customer information or publish private order and support details as part of the store’s services. Copyright reports may need to be shared with the affected party as explained in our DMCA & Copyright Policy.

5. Retention and security

We retain information for the purpose for which it was collected and for necessary accounting, legal, fraud-prevention or dispute-handling needs. Account closure does not automatically erase transaction records that must still be retained. A deletion request will be assessed against those needs; backup copies may remain until their normal replacement cycle.

Access controls and password hashing help protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password and private download links confidential. Contact us if you suspect someone has accessed your account without permission.

6. Your choices and requests

You can update available profile fields through your account. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing support@digicarts.co. Identify the email used with the store and explain the request. We may request proportionate identity verification to protect your data.

Where processing depends on consent, you can withdraw it for future processing. This may affect the related service but does not remove records we must lawfully keep. Applicable rights and response requirements depend on the law in force and the circumstances of your request.

7. Children

The store is intended for purchases by adults able to enter a contract. We do not invite children to create accounts or submit personal information independently. A parent or guardian who believes a child has supplied information should contact us so we can assess its removal.
