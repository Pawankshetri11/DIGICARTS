DigiCarts respects the rights of creators. This page explains how to report suspected copyright infringement in material offered, displayed or linked on our website. Indian law and any other applicable copyright law remain relevant; a DMCA reference does not make every dispute subject to U.S. law.

1. Send a copyright report

Email support@digicarts.co with the subject Copyright notice – DigiCarts. This is our copyright-support contact; it is not represented as a registered U.S. designated agent. If you need to serve a formal U.S. DMCA notice, confirm the appropriate designated-agent details first.

Your report should include:

  • Your name, postal address, telephone number and email.
  • The copyrighted work and evidence or a reference identifying it.
  • The specific DigiCarts URLs or file references you want reviewed.
  • Your good-faith statement that the disputed use lacks permission from the owner or agent and is not permitted by law.
  • A statement that your information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you own the relevant rights or may act for their owner.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

2. Our review

We assess reports, request missing information when needed and may restrict disputed material during review. Where appropriate, we contact the person responsible for the material. We may share relevant report details with that person to handle the dispute; do not include unrelated sensitive information.

3. Disputing a removal

If you believe removal was mistaken, contact us with the affected URL and supporting information. Where the U.S. statutory counter-notice process applies, it requires a signature, identification and former location of the material, a good-faith mistake statement under penalty of perjury, contact details, the relevant federal-court jurisdiction consent and agreement to accept service.

Under that applicable process, restoration generally falls within 10–14 business days after a compliant counter-notice, unless notice of the required court action is received. An informal disagreement does not automatically trigger this timetable. See the official Section 512 text.

4. Responsible reporting

Consider permission, ownership and lawful exceptions before reporting. Deliberately false claims may have legal consequences. We may restrict repeat infringing activity or misuse of the reporting process, consistent with applicable law. General guidance is available from the U.S. Copyright Office.

5. Other enquiries

For purchases, download problems or refund requests, use our Contact Us page or Refund Policy. Sending a copyright report does not itself cancel an order or decide refund eligibility.
