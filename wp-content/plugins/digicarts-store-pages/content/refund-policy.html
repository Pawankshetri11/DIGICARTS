Digital purchases should match what was promised. If something goes wrong with your DigiCarts order, email support@digicarts.co or use our contact form. This policy does not limit any mandatory consumer rights.

1. When to contact us

  • A paid, eligible download has not been provided or cannot be accessed.
  • The supplied file is corrupt, incomplete or unusable under its stated requirements.
  • The product is materially different from its description or advertised features.
  • You were charged twice for the same intended purchase.
  • Another payment or delivery problem needs investigation.

Let us know as soon as reasonably possible. A download alone does not remove your rights if the product is defective, deficient or misdescribed. We do not impose a short reporting deadline that overrides statutory rights.

2. Information to include

Send your order reference, the email used at checkout, the product name and a description of the problem. Relevant screenshots, error messages or a duplicate transaction reference can help. Explain whether you prefer assistance, a replacement or a refund.

Do not send passwords, complete card details, CVVs, UPI PINs or banking OTPs. If we need to verify an order, we will request information proportionate to that purpose.

3. How a request is handled

We review the purchase and the reported issue. Depending on the circumstances and your legal rights, we may restore access, provide a corrected file, offer a suitable replacement with your agreement or refund the affected purchase. A troubleshooting step will not be used to deny a refund you are legally entitled to.

Consumer complaints will be acknowledged within 48 hours and addressed within one month of receipt. We will provide a record of your complaint and explain the outcome or any information needed. These response commitments do not replace a shorter deadline required by applicable law.

4. Change of mind and compatibility

Digital files cannot be physically returned. Change-of-mind, accidental-selection or preference-based requests are considered individually, subject to applicable cancellation and consumer rights. They are not automatically accepted or automatically rejected just because a file has been downloaded.

If your software does not meet clearly stated requirements, contact us to discuss the issue. If compatibility was inaccurately described or an essential requirement was omitted, we will assess that as a product-description problem.

5. Refund payments

Approved refunds will normally go back through the original payment method, unless another lawful method is agreed. We will confirm the amount and initiation status. The payment provider or bank determines when the credit appears; we cannot guarantee a particular banking timeline.

Refunds reflect the amount actually paid for the affected item, including applicable adjustments for discounts and taxes. A refund for one item does not automatically cancel unrelated items in an order. Contact us if a confirmed refund is delayed so we can help trace it.

6. Access after a refund

For an item fully refunded and cancelled, its download permission or licence may end in accordance with the applicable licence and law. If so, stop using and distributing the refunded files and remove retained copies, except records or copies you are legally entitled to retain. Unaffected purchases remain separate.

7. Escalation

If you disagree with an outcome, reply to support@digicarts.co with your original order and complaint references and ask for a further review. This does not restrict your right to raise a payment dispute or approach a competent consumer authority.
